# 5. Typography

For a professional implementation, I recommend using **two complementary fonts**.

## English — Primary

### **Montserrat**

Use:

* `Montserrat SemiBold 600` → ATHAR
* `Montserrat Medium 500` → Intelligence
* `Montserrat Regular 400` → body text

Why?

Montserrat has the geometric, technological appearance that fits the logo extremely well.

Example:

**ATHAR**

**INTELLIGENCE**

---

## Arabic — Primary

### **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**

Recommended weights:

* `700` → headings / logo supporting text
* `600` → subheadings
* `400` → body

It gives the Arabic identity a modern technology-oriented appearance without looking overly decorative.

Example:

**أثر الذكاء**

This is my preferred pairing:

> **Montserrat + IBM Plex Sans Arabic**

# 9. Clear Space

Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo.

A simple rule:

**Clear space = height of the "A" in ATHAR**

Nothing should enter this area.

Avoid placing the logo directly against:

* photographs
* complex patterns
* UI elements
* other logos

---

# 10. Minimum Size

For digital:

**Full logo:** minimum ~`140px` wide

**Symbol:** minimum ~`24px`

For print:

**Full logo:** minimum ~`30mm` wide

For very small applications, use the **symbol-only version**.

---

# 11. Logo Don'ts

Never:

❌ Stretch the logo
❌ Squash the logo
❌ Rotate the logo
❌ Change the gold to random colors
❌ Add shadows to the corporate logo
❌ Add unnecessary gradients
❌ Put the logo over a busy photograph
❌ Change the typography
❌ Rearrange the logo elements randomly
❌ Add effects such as bevels/glows to the official version
